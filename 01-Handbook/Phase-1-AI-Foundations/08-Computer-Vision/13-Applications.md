# 📖 Introduction

Every day, millions of people use Computer Vision without realizing it.

When you:

- Unlock your phone using Face ID
- Shop using a self-checkout machine
- Drive a modern car with lane assistance
- Upload photos that automatically tag friends
- Visit a hospital for an MRI scan

Computer Vision is working behind the scenes.

Computer Vision enables machines to understand and interpret visual information from images and videos, allowing them to make intelligent decisions.

Today, Computer Vision is transforming almost every industry by improving efficiency, reducing costs, enhancing safety, and enabling automation.

# 🤔 Why is Computer Vision Important?

Visual information makes up a large portion of the data generated worldwide.

Computer Vision helps organizations:

- Automate repetitive tasks
- Improve decision-making
- Reduce human error
- Increase productivity
- Enhance customer experiences
- Improve safety

Without Computer Vision, many modern AI-powered systems would not exist.

# ⚠️ Common Beginner Mistakes

### ❌ Mistake 1

Thinking Computer Vision is only used for image classification.

✅ **Correct**

Computer Vision includes many tasks such as object detection, segmentation, face recognition, tracking, pose estimation, and image generation.

---

### ❌ Mistake 2

Believing Computer Vision only works with photos.

✅ **Correct**

It processes images, videos, medical scans, satellite imagery, and live camera feeds.

---

### ❌ Mistake 3

Assuming Computer Vision replaces humans completely.

✅ **Correct**

In many industries, Computer Vision assists experts by improving speed and accuracy rather than replacing human decision-making.

---

### ❌ Mistake 4

Thinking Computer Vision is limited to technology companies.

✅ **Correct**

It is widely used in healthcare, agriculture, banking, manufacturing, transportation, education, entertainment, logistics, and government.
