# 🧠 What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

Examples:

- Learning
- Reasoning
- Problem Solving
- Decision Making
- Language Understanding

---

# 🎯 Goal of AI

```text
Make machines capable of performing intelligent tasks.
```

---

# 📚 Important Definitions

| Term | Meaning |
|------|---------|
| AI | Machines performing intelligent tasks |
| ML | AI that learns from data |
| DL | ML using neural networks |
| GenAI | AI that creates content |
| LLM | Language-focused Generative AI model |
| Model | Learns patterns from data |
| Training | Teaching the model using data |
| Prediction | AI output |

# 🏁 Interview Closing Statement

> Artificial Intelligence enables machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence. It learns from data, identifies patterns, and makes predictions or decisions. AI is transforming industries such as healthcare, finance, education, and retail. While it offers speed, automation, and accuracy, it still depends on quality data and human oversight.
