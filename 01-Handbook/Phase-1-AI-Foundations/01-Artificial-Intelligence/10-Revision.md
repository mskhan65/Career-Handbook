# 📖 20 Key Definitions

| No. | Term | Definition |
|----|------|------------|
| 1 | Artificial Intelligence | Technology that enables machines to perform tasks requiring human intelligence. |
| 2 | Human Intelligence | Natural ability to learn, reason, and solve problems. |
| 3 | Data | Information used to train AI systems. |
| 4 | Model | A mathematical system that learns patterns from data. |
| 5 | Training | The process of teaching an AI model using data. |
| 6 | Prediction | The output generated by an AI model. |
| 7 | Pattern Recognition | Finding relationships in data. |
| 8 | Machine Learning | AI that learns from data. |
| 9 | Deep Learning | Machine Learning using multi-layer neural networks. |
|10 | Neural Network | A computing model inspired by the human brain. |
|11 | Generative AI | AI that creates new content. |
|12 | Large Language Model (LLM) | A language-focused Generative AI model trained on massive text datasets. |
|13 | Automation | Using technology to perform tasks automatically. |
|14 | Bias | Unfair influence in data or AI decisions. |
|15 | Ethics | Principles for responsible AI use. |
|16 | Recommendation System | AI that suggests products or content. |
|17 | Chatbot | AI software that communicates with users. |
|18 | Computer Vision | AI that understands images and videos. |
|19 | Natural Language Processing (NLP) | AI that enables computers to understand human language. |
|20 | AI Application | A real-world use of AI to solve practical problems. |

# ❓ Multiple Choice Questions (MCQs)

### 1. What is the primary goal of AI?

A. Replace all humans  
B. Perform tasks requiring human intelligence  
C. Build websites  
D. Store files

✅ **Answer:** B

---

### 2. Machine Learning is:

A. A type of database  
B. A subset of AI  
C. A programming language  
D. An operating system

✅ **Answer:** B

---

### 3. Deep Learning is based on:

A. SQL Databases  
B. Neural Networks  
C. HTML  
D. Excel

✅ **Answer:** B

---

### 4. Which technology creates new content?

A. Robotics  
B. Generative AI  
C. Cloud Computing  
D. Networking

✅ **Answer:** B

---

### 5. ChatGPT is powered by:

A. Spreadsheet software  
B. Large Language Models  
C. Antivirus software  
D. Operating systems

✅ **Answer:** B
