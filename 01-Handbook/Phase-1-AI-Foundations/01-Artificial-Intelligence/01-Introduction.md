# 📖 Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is one of the most exciting and transformative fields in Computer Science. Its goal is to create systems that can perform tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, understanding language, recognizing images, and making decisions.

Today, AI is no longer limited to research laboratories or science fiction movies. It has become a part of our everyday lives. Whether you unlock your phone using Face ID, receive movie recommendations on Netflix, ask ChatGPT a question, or navigate with Google Maps, you are already interacting with AI.

AI is changing the way people work, learn, communicate, shop, travel, and make decisions. Businesses use AI to automate repetitive tasks, improve customer experiences, analyze large amounts of data, and make faster, more informed decisions.

Because of its wide range of applications, AI has become one of the most valuable skills in today's technology-driven world.

# 💼 Business Perspective

Imagine a company receives thousands of customer support requests every day.

Without AI:

- Employees answer every question manually.
- Customers wait longer for responses.
- Operational costs increase.

With AI:

- Chatbots answer common questions instantly.
- Customer support teams focus on complex issues.
- Businesses save time and reduce costs.
- Customers receive faster service.

This is one reason why companies around the world are investing heavily in Artificial Intelligence.
