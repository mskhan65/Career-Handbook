# Lesson 2 – What is Artificial Intelligence?

## Question 1
### What is Artificial Intelligence?

**Answer:**

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a branch of computer science that focuses on creating systems capable of performing tasks that normally require human intelligence, such as learning, reasoning, problem-solving, decision-making, understanding language, and recognizing patterns.

---

## Question 2
### How is Artificial Intelligence different from traditional computer programming?

**Answer:**

Traditional computer programs follow explicit rules written by programmers to produce results. Artificial Intelligence learns patterns from training data instead of relying only on predefined rules. After training, AI uses those learned patterns to make predictions or decisions on new information.

**Traditional Programming:**

Data + Rules → Answer

**Artificial Intelligence (Machine Learning):**

Data + Answers → Learns Rules

# Lesson 4 – Human vs AI

## Question 1
### What are the main differences between Human Intelligence and Artificial Intelligence?

**Answer:**

Human Intelligence is the natural ability of humans to think, learn, reason, solve problems, and understand emotions through life experiences. Artificial Intelligence is a human-made technology that enables machines to perform tasks requiring intelligence by learning patterns from training data. Humans have emotions, creativity, and consciousness, while AI relies on data, algorithms, and pattern recognition.

---

## Question 2
### Can AI completely replace humans? Explain your answer.

**Answer:**

No, AI cannot completely replace humans. AI can perform many tasks faster and more efficiently, but it does not possess real emotions, consciousness, common sense, or human life experiences. AI assists humans by automating repetitive tasks and supporting decision-making, while humans remain responsible for creativity, ethics, and complex judgment.


# Lesson 5 – How AI Works

---

## Question 1

**Q:** Explain how AI works from start to finish.

**Answer:**

```text
Data
   ↓
Data Preparation
   ↓
Training
   ↓
Pattern Learning
   ↓
AI Model
   ↓
New Input
   ↓
Prediction / Decision
   ↓
Output
```

AI first gathers data. The data is then cleaned and organized because it may contain errors, missing values, or duplicates. During training, the AI learns patterns from the prepared data and creates a trained model. When new input is provided, the model uses the learned patterns to make a prediction or decision and produces an output.

## Question 2

**Q:** Why is data preparation important before training an AI model?

**Answer:**

Data preparation ensures the data is clean, complete, and well-organized before training. Since real-world data often contains missing values, errors, or duplicate records, cleaning it helps the AI learn accurate patterns and improves the quality of its predictions.

# Lesson 6 – AI Hierarchy

---

## Question 1

**Q:** What is the AI Hierarchy?

**Answer:**

```text
Artificial Intelligence (AI)
        │
        ▼
Machine Learning (ML)
        │
        ▼
Deep Learning (DL)
        │
        ▼
Generative AI
        │
        ▼
Large Language Models (LLMs)
```

The AI hierarchy shows the relationship between AI technologies. AI is the broadest field, and each level below it is a subset of the previous one.
