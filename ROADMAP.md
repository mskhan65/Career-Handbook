# 🗺️ The AI Generalist Roadmap

> A complete roadmap to becoming an AI Generalist.

---

## 📖 Introduction

Welcome to **The AI Generalist Roadmap**.

This roadmap provides a structured learning journey from AI fundamentals to building production-ready AI systems, products, and businesses.

Unlike traditional AI roadmaps that focus on only one area, this roadmap is designed to help you develop a broad and practical understanding of the modern AI ecosystem.

Whether you're a student, developer, entrepreneur, freelancer, or business professional, this roadmap will guide you step by step toward becoming an **AI Generalist**.

---

## 🎯 Goal

By following this roadmap, you will learn how to:

- 🧠 Understand Artificial Intelligence
- 🤖 Build AI-powered applications
- 💬 Master Prompt Engineering
- 🧩 Develop AI Agents
- 🔍 Build RAG systems
- 🌐 Create AI websites and applications
- 📱 Develop AI mobile apps
- ⚡ Automate workflows with AI
- 🎨 Build multimodal AI applications
- 🚀 Launch AI products and SaaS
- 💼 Solve real-world business problems using AI
- 📂 Build a professional AI portfolio

The destination isn't just learning AI—it's becoming someone who can confidently use, build, deploy, and continuously learn AI technologies.


---

# 🎓 Complete Learning Journey

The AI Generalist Handbook is divided into **16 progressive phases**. Each phase builds upon the previous one, gradually transforming you from a beginner into a confident AI Generalist capable of building real-world AI solutions.

| Phase | Focus | Outcome |
|--------|-------|---------|
| 🌱 Phase 1 | AI Foundations | Understand the core concepts of Artificial Intelligence and Machine Learning. |
| 🚀 Phase 2 | AI Productivity | Learn to use modern AI tools to improve productivity and daily workflows. |
| 💬 Phase 3 | Prompt Engineering & Modern LLMs | Master prompt engineering and understand how Large Language Models work. |
| 🤖 Phase 4 | AI Agents | Build intelligent AI agents capable of reasoning, planning, and tool usage. |
| 🎨 Phase 5 | Multimodal AI | Develop applications using text, images, audio, video, and speech AI. |
| 💻 Phase 6 | AI Development | Learn the technologies required to build AI-powered applications. |
| 🌐 Phase 7 | AI Applications | Create websites, mobile apps, chatbots, assistants, and AI SaaS products. |
| ⚙️ Phase 8 | Automation & AI Ops | Automate workflows and deploy production-ready AI systems. |
| 🏢 Phase 9 | Business AI | Apply AI to marketing, sales, finance, education, healthcare, and business operations. |
| 📊 Phase 10 | Data & Analytics | Work with data, SQL, visualization, analytics, and business intelligence. |
| 🔒 Phase 11 | AI Security & Responsible AI | Learn AI security, privacy, governance, safety, and responsible AI practices. |
| ☁️ Phase 12 | Cloud & Deployment | Deploy, scale, and maintain AI applications using modern cloud platforms. |
| 🧪 Phase 13 | Advanced AI | Explore fine-tuning, open-source models, local LLMs, evaluation, and cutting-edge AI topics. |
| 💼 Phase 14 | Career & Portfolio | Build your portfolio, resume, GitHub profile, personal brand, and prepare for AI careers. |
| 🚀 Phase 15 | Capstone Projects | Build complete end-to-end AI projects that showcase your skills. |
| 📐 Phase 16 | Mathematics for AI *(Optional)* | Strengthen your understanding of the mathematics behind modern AI and Machine Learning. |

---

> **By completing all phases, you'll gain the knowledge, practical skills, and confidence to build AI-powered products, solve real-world problems, and continuously adapt to the rapidly evolving AI landscape.**
