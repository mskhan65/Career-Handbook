# Career Roadmap

## Long-Term Goal

Become an **AI-Native Creative Solutions Builder**.

The goal is not to collect technologies.

The goal is to become a technically strong, commercially useful builder who can:

- Design
- Develop
- Automate
- Solve business problems
- Build digital products
- Use AI effectively
- Create AI-powered solutions

---

---

# How Progress Works

The phases are **not equal in length**.

Progress is based on:

- Competency
- Projects
- Real-world work
- Problem-solving ability
- Commercial capability
- Technical depth
- Ability to work independently

A phase is not completed simply because a course was completed.

---

# Commercial Skills vs Technical Spine

Some phases exist primarily to increase commercial versatility:

- Framer
- Webflow
- Figma
- WordPress

The deeper technical spine is:

**HTML/CSS → JavaScript → TypeScript/React/Next.js → Full-Stack → Automation → Python/AI → RAG/Agents → AI Solutions**

Both sides are important.

The commercial side helps create opportunities.

The technical side raises the long-term ceiling.

---

# AI-Native Development Philosophy

AI is used throughout the journey.

The goal is not:

> "Let AI do everything."

The goal is:

**AI generates → I inspect → I understand → I debug → I test → I improve → I ship**

AI should increase speed without destroying understanding.

---

# Career Strategy

The roadmap is designed to keep multiple career paths open:

### Path A — Freelancing

Build websites, applications, automations, and AI solutions for clients.

### Path B — Remote Employment

Develop enough technical depth to compete for frontend, full-stack, automation, and AI-related roles.

### Path C — Agency / Partnerships

Combine design, development, automation, and AI capabilities with other specialists.

### Path D — Products

Eventually create digital products, SaaS, AI tools, and business solutions.

---

# Core Principle

**Build capability first.**

Certificates, tutorials, tools, and courses are supporting resources.

Projects and real-world results are the primary evidence.
